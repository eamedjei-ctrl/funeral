Our Calling & Heritage

A Ghanaian Home for Dignity & Heartfelt Care

Memorial Planner Funeral Services (MPFS) is a fully integrated, one-stop funeral planning and memorial event management company based in Lashibi, Accra — serving Ghanaian families at home and across the diaspora, 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Our vision is to become one of Ghana's most trusted and respected funeral service providers — known globally for excellence, dignity, professionalism and compassionate care. Our mission is to provide complete, seamless funeral and memorial solutions that bring comfort, peace of mind and honourable farewells to families during difficult times.
